How It Started

I'm Aidan Copeland. Back in April 2019, while scrolling through YouTube, I came across a custom sneaker video by an artist named Sierato — stencils, airbrushes, masking tape, toothbrushes turning a blank shoe into something else entirely. I'd always loved art, and with 8th grade graduation two months out, I set myself a deadline: finish a pair of my own in time to wear it.

A pair of Vans. A 12-pack of Angelus paint. Months of trial and error using the techniques I'd watched on repeat. On the morning of graduation, they were finished; not identical to Sierato's, but entirely my own.

What started as one pair for myself became one for a friend, then teammates, then customers I'd never met. Cope Custom Kicks was never planned as a business, but it became one, one commission at a time.

Build a Pair

Two ways to start.

PATH 01

You Design It

Come with a detailed theme. You'll get a price built around exactly what you describe.

  1. 01Send reference images, logos, or a written brief of exactly what you want on the shoe.
  2. 02Get a fixed price back within a day or two — no back-and-forth needed since the design is already locked.
  3. 03Approve it, send a deposit, and the base shoe goes into production.
PATH 02

We Design It Together

Bring a basic theme and a budget. A design gets built to match both.

  1. 01Share a loose idea — a team, a person, a vibe — and a budget you're comfortable with.
  2. 02A concept sketch comes back built to fit both the theme and the price.
  3. 03Tweak it if needed, then it's approved and goes into production.
